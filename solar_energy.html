Solar Energy

A Clean and Renewable Source of Power

Introduction

As the world's energy demands continue to rise, renewable energy sources have become increasingly important. Solar energy is one of the most abundant and eco-friendly sources of power available today. It is generated by converting sunlight into electricity using solar panels. Solar energy helps reduce dependence on fossil fuels, lowers electricity costs, and contributes to environmental sustainability. Governments and organizations worldwide are encouraging the adoption of solar technology to build a cleaner and greener future.

Benefits of Solar Energy

    • 1. Renewable Energy Source Solar energy is an unlimited resource because sunlight is available every day. Unlike fossil fuels, it does not get depleted over time.
      2. Environment Friendly Solar power generates electricity without producing harmful greenhouse gases or air pollution, helping reduce global warming.
      3. Lower Electricity Bills Installing solar panels allows homes and businesses to generate their own electricity, reducing monthly electricity expenses.
      4. Low Maintenance Solar panels require minimal maintenance and can operate efficiently for more than 25 years with periodic cleaning.
